Welcome to your wiki on Cloudflare.

* ''Where saves go:'' every tiddler you save goes to the bucket at once, and to GitHub once backups are set up ([[setup 2.4|https://github.com/DesignThinkerer/tiddlywiki-cloudflare/blob/main/docs/setup.md#24-the-backup-repository-optional]]). There's no file to save by hand.
* ''The cloud icon'' in the sidebar turns red while something waits to be sent; click it to see why (offline, or your login expired).
* ''The setup'' is checked from <$link to="$:/ControlPanel">Control Panel</$link> → Saving → Cloudflare, //Check the setup//: each piece set up by hand, with what to do about a missing one ([[operating 3.15|https://github.com/DesignThinkerer/tiddlywiki-cloudflare/blob/main/docs/operating.md#315-checking-the-setup]]).
