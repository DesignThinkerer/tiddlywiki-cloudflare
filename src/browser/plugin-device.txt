What this browser keeps of the wiki, for opening and editing offline ([[operating 3.13|https://github.com/DesignThinkerer/tiddlywiki-cloudflare/blob/main/docs/operating.md#313-opening-the-wiki-offline]]): the device copy (the tiddlers loaded here), the outbox (the changes not yet sent) and the service worker's files. Nothing here asks the worker anything.

! Keeping it

A browser short of space may clear a site's storage, a change waiting in the outbox with it, unless it has promised to keep it. Firefox asks you; Chrome and Safari decide by their own rules (a site used often, installed or bookmarked) and answer at once.

! Forgetting it

Removes the device copy, the outbox and the service worker's files from this browser, then reloads: the wiki comes back from the worker and the copy fills again, as on a new device. Refused while a change or a draft waits in the outbox, and while the worker doesn't answer (the page comes back from it once the copy is gone).
